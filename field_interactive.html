The Field

A live model of the Intelligence System — five surfaces distributed across the interface, unified by a single responsive layer that reads visitor intent and delivers guidance in real time.

What It Is

The Field is not a single feature — it is an architectural layer. What visitors experience as a search bar, a notification badge, and a chat window is, structurally, one coherent intelligence surface operating across four zones. Each surface is an entry point into the same underlying system.

Zone A · Nav Bar
AI Search captures intent and activates The Field. Notifications reflect ambient system awareness — shifting between Idle, Aware, and Live.
Zone B · Filter Bar
Filtered Content shapes what The Field prioritizes without navigating away — by park, season, activity, or inferred intent.
Zone C · Flyout Panel
The Notification Center delivers proactive intelligence — alerts, curated pathways, guidance — as a persistent surface on every page.
Zone D · Footer
AI Chat opens conversational depth for complex planning, nuanced queries, and guided multi-park discovery.
